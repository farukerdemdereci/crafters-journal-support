Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

Crafter’s Journal is an independent third-party companion application for Albion Online. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

Crafter’s Journal does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

App preferences

Preferences such as your selected server and language may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your settings.

Market and crafting data

The app retrieves market, historical, crafting, and related calculated data required to provide its features. Community-sourced Albion Online market data is provided through the Albion Online Data Project.

Server and network logs

When the app communicates with online services, standard technical information may be processed automatically by hosting or infrastructure providers, such as IP address, request time, device/network information, and error or security logs. Such information is used only as necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the service.

Advertising and tracking

Crafter’s Journal does not contain advertising and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Third-party services

The app relies on third-party infrastructure and community market-data services to provide functionality. Those services may process technical data according to their own policies.

Children

Crafter’s Journal does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
