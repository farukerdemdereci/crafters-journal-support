Common questions

Why can market prices differ from the game?

Market information is community-sourced and may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable for some items, cities, or servers.

Is Crafter’s Journal an official Albion Online app?

No. Crafter’s Journal is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or otherwise associated with Sandbox Interactive GmbH.

Where does the market data come from?

Community-sourced Albion Online market information is provided through the Albion Online Data Project.

Can I change my server later?

Yes. You can change the selected Albion server from the app’s Settings screen.
